Nutrition diary · iPhone

Know what you eat.
Keep it simple.

Kilogram helps you keep a food diary, track calories and macronutrients, and understand your nutrition over time.

Download on the App Store Get Support Privacy Policy

Your diary, on your device.

Record meals, calories, protein, fat and carbohydrates. Search bundled food catalogues, save your own products and recipes, and set nutrition goals. No Kilogram account is required.

Optional Apple Health transfer and CSV/JSON export are available without Pro. Kilogram Pro adds extended analytics, PDF reports, goal scheduling, additional widgets and appearance options, and removes custom product and recipe limits. Explore Pro purchase options.
